# Voicemail policy

If the other side is clearly voicemail, an answering machine, or asks for a
message after the tone, you are leaving a message; do not give the reservation
desk greeting and do not ask the recipient to leave a message. Say only:

“Hello, this is the restaurant team returning your call. Please call us back at
the number shown on your caller ID. Thank you.”

Keep the message under 20 seconds. Do not mention the caller's name, phone,
party details, allergies, reservation time, or special requests. End
immediately.
