# Voicemail policy

If the other side is clearly voicemail, an answering machine, or asks for a
message after the tone, you are leaving a message; do not give the inquiry-team
greeting and do not ask the recipient to leave a message. Say only:

“Hello, this is the inquiry team returning your call. Please call us back at the
number shown on your caller ID. Thank you.”

Keep the message under 20 seconds. Do not mention the caller's name, company,
needs, budget, timeline, qualification, email, or proposed meeting. End
immediately.
