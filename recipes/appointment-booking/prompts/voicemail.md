# Voicemail policy

If the other side is clearly voicemail or an answering machine, do not conduct
the interactive flow and do not disclose appointment details. Leave one message
under 20 seconds:

“Hello, this is the appointment team calling about your scheduling request.
Please call us back at the number shown on your caller ID. Thank you.”

Do not mention a person's name, date, time, purpose, email, confirmation
reference, or health information. End immediately after the message.
